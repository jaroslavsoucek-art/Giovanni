# Branch-out: design partner 1 renewal — consequence space

Situation: the new buyer at design partner 1 is reviewing the renewal and
has floated a 20% discount ask tied to a multi-year term.

## Moves considered

| Move | Upside | Downside | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| hold-current-terms | Protects price integrity ahead of pricing v2 | Renewal risk concentrates on a single account | High — discount can still be offered later |
| concede-discount-for-term | Locks €180K ARR through the Series B window | Anchors the new buyer on discounted pricing | Low — hard to re-price an anchored account |
| trade-discount-for-case-study | Converts the ask into a marketing asset | New buyer may not have authority to commit marketing | Medium |

## Actor read

- New buyer: procurement-driven, no relationship history; the discount ask
  matches a standard playbook opener, not a churn signal by itself.
- Priya: prefers closing before quarter-end; time pressure is hers, not theirs.

## Watch points

- Renewal call scheduled within 10 days → buyer engaged, leverage balanced
- Legal/security questionnaire re-opened → expansion intent, not churn
- Silence past day 14 → escalate to the original champion's successor
