## Communication style

**How I address her:**
- **Channel preference:** Teams 1:1 (monthly recurring), email between
  meetings for substantive items, board materials via Diligent. Avoid
  Slack — she doesn't run Lattice's workspace and asynchronous slack
  has lost context on her side before.
- **Language:** English; UK register on her side, neutral on mine.
- **Register:** professional, semi-formal. First-name basis, but no
  slang.
- **Tone notes:** pre-reads land better than verbal walkthroughs;
  structure (headings, bullets, evidence) signals respect for her
  bandwidth across 14 portcos. Hand-waving on numbers gets caught
  immediately — never extemporize unit economics in a 1:1.

**How she communicates:**
- **Response time:** same business day during UK hours for substantive
  emails; 1-2 day lag on attachments she needs to actually read.
- **Length:** brief on Slack/email when ack'ing, medium-structured when
  raising something she wants tracked; long-form only in board materials
  she authors herself.
- **Patterns:**
  - **Silence-then-unprompted-email when topic matters** — the absence of
    real-time response followed by a calm-toned email is the heads-up
    signal. The thing that gets emailed is the thing she's been thinking
    about.
  - **Adds items to standing 1:1 agenda rather than escalating ad-hoc.**
    She prefers governing through cadence than ad-hoc fire alarms.
  - **Forwards articles without commentary** = soft probe; "what's your
    take?" is implied. Responding without engaging the underlying topic
    is read as evasive.
  - **Walks back surprises later.** If something unexpected comes up in
    a meeting, expect a follow-up note clarifying her position 24-48h
    later — she does not finalize in the room.
