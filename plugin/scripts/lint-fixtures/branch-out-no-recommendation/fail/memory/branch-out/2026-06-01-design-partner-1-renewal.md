# Branch-out: design partner 1 renewal — consequence space

Situation: the new buyer at design partner 1 is reviewing the renewal and
has floated a 20% discount ask tied to a multi-year term.

## Moves considered

| Move | Upside | Downside | Recommended |
|---|---|---|---|
| hold-current-terms | Protects price integrity | Renewal risk concentrates | No |
| concede-discount-for-term | Locks ARR through Series B | Anchors discounted pricing | Yes |

## Recommendation

We recommend conceding the discount in exchange for a three-year term —
this is the best move given the Series B timing. You should open with the
case-study trade and fall back to the discount.
