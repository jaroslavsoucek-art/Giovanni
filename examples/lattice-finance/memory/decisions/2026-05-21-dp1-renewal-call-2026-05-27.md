## Decision

Run the 2026-05-27 first call with Karim as a **value-discovery and relationship-reset conversation**, not a renewal close. Anchor the agenda on understanding what Karim (the new buyer-of-record) measures and is accountable for as Helios CFO, surface the multi-entity reporting work in progress as proof of trajectory, and **do not** put a renewal number, discount, or commitment on the table in this call. Hold price and term entirely for a second call after the 2026-06-15 demo.

## Reasoning

- Karim inherited the account; the original champion who bought Lattice has left. We have no relationship equity with him and no read on what he values. Leading with a renewal ask before establishing that read risks anchoring on the wrong axis (price) when his real concern may be reliability, audit-readiness, or consolidation across Helios entities.
- The strongest renewal lever — the multi-entity reporting demo — is not ready until 2026-06-15 (per the [dp1 tech-ask scope cap](2026-05-15-dp1-tech-ask-scope-cap.md)). Pitching renewal on 2026-05-27 before the demo means pitching on the old value story, which the departed champion bought and Karim did not.
- A CFO buyer-of-record evaluating an inherited vendor is, by default, looking for a reason to cut spend. A discovery posture lets us reframe Lattice from "a line item the last person signed" to "infrastructure for the consolidation problem you own" before any number is discussed.
- The €180K ARR account is the single largest churn exposure in the book and a material input to the Series B net-retention narrative. Burning the first call on a premature pitch is a low-upside, high-regret move.

## Alternatives considered

- **Open with a renewal proposal + loyalty discount:** rejected for this call. Discounting to an unscoped buyer before demonstrating value trains Karim to treat price as the only variable and leaves no room to expand. Hold as a possible second-call instrument only if discovery surfaces a hard budget ceiling.
- **Open with a hard multi-entity demo:** deferred. Demo is not build-ready until 2026-06-15; a half-finished walkthrough on 2026-05-27 would undercut the value story rather than build it. Sequence discovery first, demo second.
- **Delay the call until after the 2026-06-15 demo:** rejected. The renewal review window is Q3 and Karim is actively forming his vendor-keep/cut list now. Going dark for three weeks cedes the framing to whatever internal champion-less narrative already exists at Helios.

## Implications

### Operational

- Alex prepares a discovery question set (Karim's KPIs, Helios entity/consolidation pain, who else at Helios touches the tool) and runs the call agenda-light, listening-heavy.
- No pricing artifact, proposal deck, or term sheet goes into the 2026-05-27 call. Second call (target week of 2026-06-15, post-demo) is where the number lands.
- Notes from the call feed straight into the dp1-renewal topic shard and update the design-partner-1 stakeholder profile (Karim's stated priorities, tone, and any churn signals).

### Stakeholder

- **Karim Solanki (buyer-of-record, inside dp1 profile):** treated as a new relationship, not a continuation. First call is about him, not the contract.
- **Sarah Vyas:** flagged at the 2026-05-26 monthly 1:1 that the dp1 first call is happening and the posture is discovery-first, so a churn signal (if it emerges) is not a board surprise.
- **Morgan Chen:** owns confirming on 2026-06-01 that the 2026-06-15 demo scope is a genuine value demonstration, since the whole sequencing rests on that demo carrying the renewal.

### Commercial

- Renewal economics (price, term, any platform-fee/per-entity migration under [pricing v2](2026-05-18-pricing-v2-platform-fee.md)) are explicitly out of scope for 2026-05-27 and parked for the post-demo second call.

## Trigger conditions for re-evaluation

- **Karim signals an active churn timeline on the 2026-05-27 call** (e.g. "we're reviewing all vendors by end of Q2"): collapse the two-call sequence — bring the renewal conversation and a retention offer forward rather than waiting for the demo.
- **2026-06-15 demo slips past 2026-06-22** (cap binds per the tech-ask scope-cap decision): the value anchor for the second call disappears; re-decide whether to lead the renewal on a different value story or escalate the contractor bridge.
- **Karim declines or repeatedly reschedules the first call:** re-read as a disengagement signal; escalate to a save-the-account play and loop Sarah in before Q3 board.
- **Discovery surfaces a hard budget cut at Helios:** reopen the discount/right-size alternative that was rejected for this call.
