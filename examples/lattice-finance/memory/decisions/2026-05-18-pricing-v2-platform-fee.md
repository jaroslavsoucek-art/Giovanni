# Decision: pricing v2 moves to platform fee + per-entity layer

## Context

Per-seat pricing does not fit the treasury-team buyer pattern — seats stay
flat while entity count grows with the customer's structure.

## Decision

Adopt platform fee + per-entity pricing for all new deals from next quarter.
Existing design partners grandfathered until renewal.

## Trigger conditions for re-evaluation

- Two consecutive lost deals citing entity-count pricing as the blocker
- Net retention drops below 105% for two consecutive quarters
- A design partner renewal stalls specifically on the per-entity layer
