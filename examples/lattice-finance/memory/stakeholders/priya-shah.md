## Communication style

**How I address her:**
- **Channel preference:** Teams for 1:1s and forecast reviews, Slack for
  async + deal-by-deal updates, in-person when she's in the office (2
  days/week). She runs the CRM as the source of truth — point her there
  rather than re-asking for pipeline status.
- **Language:** English; direct, commercial register.
- **Register:** professional but warm; first-name, no formality needed.
- **Tone notes:** she responds well to clear decision rights ("you own
  this, here's the floor, escalate above it") and badly to ambiguous
  ownership. As her manager, the highest-leverage thing is removing
  ambiguity on what she can decide vs. what comes to Alex.

**How she communicates:**
- **Response time:** fast on Slack during working hours; forecast and
  written analysis come on a weekly cadence, reliably.
- **Length:** brief on deal updates; structured when presenting a
  forecast or a pipeline view.
- **Patterns:**
  - **Under-forecasts in her first full quarters** — manages expectations
    down; read her numbers with a correction factor until a track record
    builds.
  - **Absorbs peer "no"s rather than escalating** — when Morgan or a peer
    declines a request, she tends to drop it in the room rather than route
    it to Alex. Check in afterward — silence isn't always agreement, and
    she may be under-advocating.
  - **Flags external dependencies as decisions, increasingly** — recent
    shift toward bringing Alex a framed choice ("hold or push?") rather
    than a status report. Encourage this.
