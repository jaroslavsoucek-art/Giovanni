## One-liner

Lattice is the treasury-automation layer for mid-market finance teams — cash forecasting, bank connectivity, and FX exposure across every entity, in one place.

## Why now

Mid-market companies (€10–100M revenue) run treasury across spreadsheets and a dozen bank portals. The incumbent tools are either enterprise-priced (six-figure floor, finance-team-of-twenty buyer) or consumer-grade. Nothing fits the €10–100M finance team that has 2–4 people and 5+ bank relationships.

## Traction (the numbers the round is built on)

- ARR €1.2M, up from €200K at Series A 14 months ago.
- Series B fundability gate (target by month 24): **€3M ARR + Net Retention >115%**.
- 3 design partners live; largest is €180K ARR (renewal in flight — see `memory/topics/dp1-renewal.md`).

## The wedge → platform story

1. **Wedge:** cash forecasting across multi-entity, multi-bank.
2. **Expand:** bank connectivity + FX exposure as attach modules.
3. **Platform:** pricing v2 (platform fee + per-entity) aligns price to value and lifts Net Retention — the core Series B metric (see `memory/topics/pricing-v2.md`).
